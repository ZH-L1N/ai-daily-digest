# AI Daily Digest - 2026-04-26

## Anthropic
- **[Google Commits Up to $40 Billion Investment in Anthropic](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/24/google-to-invest-up-to-40-billion-in-anthropic-as-search-giant-spreads-its-ai-bets.html)** - Google announced it will invest $10 billion in Anthropic now (at a $350B valuation), with up to $30 billion more contingent on hitting performance targets. The deal expands their existing partnership and includes a significant expansion of Anthropic's computing capacity using Google/Broadcom TPUs. Anthropic's annualized revenue has now topped $30 billion.

- **[Claude Mythos Unauthorized Access Investigation](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/21/unauthorized-group-has-gained-access-to-anthropics-exclusive-cyber-tool-mythos-report-claims/)** - A small group of users gained unauthorized access to Claude Mythos Preview — Anthropic's powerful but restricted cybersecurity AI — through a third-party vendor portal on the same day it was publicly announced. Anthropic confirmed it is investigating, stating there is no evidence its core systems were compromised; the group appears to be curiosity-driven rather than malicious.

- **[Claude Code Quality Fixes & Usage Limits Reset](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic)** - Anthropic traced three separate changes that had degraded Claude Code quality: reduced reasoning effort, a caching bug that dropped thinking history, and a verbosity prompt change. All three were fixed as of April 20 (v2.1.116) and usage limits were reset for subscribers. The latest Claude Code update also ships faster startup, stronger plugin management, and better session resume.

## OpenAI
- **[GPT-5.5 Released to Paid Users and API](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-5/)** - OpenAI launched GPT-5.5, its most capable model yet, with particular strength in agentic coding, computer use, knowledge work, and scientific research. It rolled out to Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users in ChatGPT and Codex on April 23, and became available via API on April 24 with GPT-5.5 Pro also available for power users.

- **[Workspace Agents in ChatGPT Launch](https://openai.com/index/introducing-workspace-agents-in-chatgpt/)** - OpenAI introduced Codex-powered "workspace agents" — shared, cloud-based team agents that handle complex workflows across ChatGPT and Slack. Teams can build agents once and share them organization-wide; they run on a schedule or respond to requests when you're away. Available in research preview for Business, Enterprise, Edu, and Teacher plans and free until May 6, 2026.

- **[Codex for (Almost) Everything Update](https://openai.com/index/codex-for-almost-everything/)** - The April 16 Codex update significantly expanded the platform beyond coding: it now includes computer use on macOS (with desktop agents working in parallel), an in-app browser, gpt-image-1.5 for image generation, persistent memory, scheduled automations, and 90+ plugins covering Jira, Microsoft 365, Notion, Slack, and more. OpenAI also launched Codex Labs to accelerate enterprise adoption, with weekly active developer usage growing from 3 million to 4 million in just two weeks.

## Google AI
- **[Deep Research and Deep Research Max Launch in Gemini API](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/next-generation-gemini-deep-research/)** - Google unveiled two new autonomous research agents built on Gemini 3.1 Pro: "Deep Research" (optimized for low-latency interactive use) and "Deep Research Max" (uses extended test-time compute for exhaustive overnight batch analysis). Both support MCP for connecting to internal enterprise data sources and natively generate charts and infographics; Deep Research scored 85.9 on BrowseComp, more than 25 points above the December predecessor. Available in public preview via the Gemini API (not yet in the consumer Gemini app).

- **[Google Expands Enterprise AI Partnerships at Cloud Next '26](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2026/accenture-and-google-cloud-expand-partnership-to-scale-agentic-transformation-for-global-enterprises-with-gemini-enterprise)** - At Cloud Next '26 (April 22), both Accenture and Deloitte announced major expansions of their Gemini Enterprise partnerships, building dedicated agentic transformation practices. Deloitte has deployed Gemini Enterprise to 25,000+ professionals (with plans to reach 100,000) and built a library of 1,000+ industry-specific AI agents; Accenture's program brings thousands of AI engineers for enterprise-scale deployments.

## Developer Tools & Vibe Coding
- **[SpaceX Secures Option to Acquire Cursor AI for $60 Billion](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/21/spacex-is-working-with-cursor-and-has-an-option-to-buy-the-startup-for-60-billion/)** - SpaceX announced a partnership with Cursor (Anysphere) to build "the world's best coding and knowledge work AI," with an option to acquire Cursor for $60 billion later this year or pay $10 billion for their joint work together. The deal was struck just as Cursor was about to close a $2B fundraise, and gives Cursor access to SpaceX/xAI's massive Colossus supercomputer (1M H100-equivalent GPUs) to advance its Composer 2 agentic coding model.

- **[DeepSeek V4 Open-Sourced with 1M Context at ~1/9 the Price of GPT-5.5](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/24/deepseek-v4-llm-preview-open-source-ai-competition-china.html)** - Chinese AI lab DeepSeek released preview versions of DeepSeek-V4-Pro (1.6T parameters, 49B active) and V4-Flash (284B total, 13B active) on April 24 under Apache 2.0. Both support 1 million token contexts via a new Hybrid Attention architecture that reduces KV cache to ~10% of V3.2 at 1M tokens. V4-Pro output pricing is $3.48/M tokens versus $30 for GPT-5.5; V4 is already ranked #1 on the Vibe Code Benchmark among open-weight models.
