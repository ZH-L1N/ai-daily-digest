# AI Daily Digest - 2026-04-28

## Anthropic

- **[Claude Will Remain Ad-Free](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-is-a-space-to-think)** - Anthropic formally published "Claude is a space to think," committing that Claude will carry no ads, no sponsored links, and no third-party product placements ever. The announcement is an explicit positioning move against OpenAI, which recently rolled out ads for ChatGPT Free and Go-tier users in several English-speaking markets. Anthropic argues advertising incentives are fundamentally incompatible with a genuinely helpful AI assistant, and plans to expand access via subscriptions and enterprise contracts instead.

- **[Anthropic Opens Sydney Office, Names ANZ General Manager](https://www.anthropic.com/news)** - On April 27, Anthropic officially opened its Sydney office and named Theo Hourmouzis as General Manager for Australia & New Zealand. The move follows an earlier MOU with the Australian Government and a commitment of AUD $3M in Claude API credits to Australian research institutions, cementing Anthropic's permanent presence in the APAC region.

- **[Claude Mythos Unauthorized Access Investigation Ongoing](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/21/unauthorized-group-has-gained-access-to-anthropics-exclusive-cyber-tool-mythos-report-claims/)** - Anthropic confirmed it is investigating a report of unauthorized access to Claude Mythos Preview through a third-party vendor environment. A small group on a private forum reportedly guessed the model's endpoint on the day of announcement, aided by credentials from a contractor and data from a breach at AI recruiting startup Mercor. Anthropic says it has found no evidence the activity impacted its core systems, but the incident raises serious questions about the security of its Project Glasswing limited-access rollout.

- **[Claude Cowork Now Available in Amazon Bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/aws-weekly-roundup-anthropic-meta-partnership-aws-lambda-s3-files-amazon-bedrock-agentcore-cli-and-more-april-27-2026/)** - This week, AWS and Anthropic deepened their partnership: Anthropic is now training its most advanced models on AWS Trainium and Graviton silicon, co-engineering at the chip level with Annapurna Labs. Claude Cowork is also now deployable directly within Amazon Bedrock, letting enterprise teams leverage Cowork's collaborative workflows inside AWS while keeping data within their own cloud environment.

## OpenAI

- **[WSJ: OpenAI Missed Internal Sales and User Targets](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-28/openai-misses-its-own-user-and-sales-goals-wsj-reports)** - Today's biggest market-moving story: the Wall Street Journal reported that OpenAI fell short of several monthly sales targets in 2026 after rival Anthropic gained ground in coding and enterprise markets. CFO Sarah Friar privately warned that ballooning compute costs—locked in through roughly $600 billion in future data-center commitments—could outpace revenue growth. OpenAI also missed its end-of-2025 goal of reaching 1 billion weekly active ChatGPT users. SoftBank tumbled nearly 10% in Tokyo on the news, while CoreWeave and Oracle fell over 7% in US premarket trading. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman and Friar issued a joint statement saying they are "totally aligned on buying as much compute as we can."

- **[OpenAI Publishes "Our Principles"](https://openai.com/news/)** - On April 26, OpenAI published a public principles document describing its belief that AI can significantly improve society while also concentrating power if not handled carefully. The document covers democratization, empowerment, and universal prosperity, and represents the company's most explicit public statement of its ethical and political theory for frontier AI deployment. Analysts view the release as both a governance maturity signal and a competitive move that pressures rivals to articulate their own values with equal clarity.

- **[GPT-5.5 Rolls Out to Paid Subscribers](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/23/openai-announces-latest-artificial-intelligence-model.html)** - OpenAI released GPT-5.5 on April 23 to Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users in ChatGPT and Codex, just six weeks after GPT-5.4. The model excels at agentic coding, computer use, and knowledge work; it achieves 82.7% accuracy on Terminal-Bench 2.0 and is state-of-the-art on SWE-Bench Pro. OpenAI noted 4 million active Codex users and more than 85% of its own staff using Codex weekly. API access is coming "very soon" with additional safeguards.

## Google AI

- **[Google Cloud Next '26: Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform & TPU v8](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/google-cloud/next-2026/)** - At Google Cloud Next 2026 (April 22–24, Las Vegas), Google launched the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform—a full end-to-end workspace to build, govern, scale, and optimize AI agents using Gemini 3.1 Pro, Gemini 3.1 Flash Image, and Lyria 3. The platform also adds Anthropic Claude Opus 4.7 as an available model. Google also unveiled eighth-generation TPUs in two variants: Sunfish (large-scale training) and Zebrafish (low-latency inference). Sundar Pichai noted that 75% of all new code at Google is now AI-generated, and first-party API throughput has reached 16 billion tokens per minute.

- **[Deep Research and Deep Research Max Launch on Gemini API](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/next-generation-gemini-deep-research/)** - Google DeepMind introduced two new autonomous research agents built on Gemini 3.1 Pro: the speed-optimized Deep Research and the comprehensive Deep Research Max, which uses extended test-time compute for maximum synthesis quality. Both agents support MCP for secure connection to private enterprise data sources, and generate native charts and infographics inline. Gemini 3.1 Pro scores 85.9 on OpenAI's BrowseComp benchmark (vs 60-something for Gemini 3 Pro), a 25+ point jump. The agents are available now in the Gemini API under model IDs `deep-research-preview-04-2026` and `deep-research-max-preview-04-2026`.

- **[Google Cloud Commits $750M to Accelerate Partner Agentic AI](https://www.googlecloudpresscorner.com/2026-04-22-Google-Cloud-Commits-750-Million-to-Accelerate-Partners-Agentic-AI-Development)** - At Cloud Next '26, Google Cloud announced a $750 million fund for its 120,000-member partner ecosystem to accelerate agentic AI deployments. The fund supports consulting firms like Accenture, Deloitte, and McKinsey with AI value assessments, Gemini proofs-of-concept, embedded forward-deployed engineers, and early model access. Agents from Adobe, Atlassian, Replit, Salesforce, ServiceNow, and Workday are now surfaced directly in the Gemini Enterprise app.

## Developer Tools & Vibe Coding

- **[Amazon Bedrock AgentCore Gets Managed Harness, CLI & Coding Skills](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/04/agentcore-new-features-to-build-agents-faster/)** - AWS announced new AgentCore features that replace a days-long agent setup with a minutes-long process: a managed agent harness (preview) that handles orchestration, compute, tool connections, and session state automatically; an AgentCore CLI for end-to-end lifecycle management from a single terminal; and pre-built AgentCore skills for Kiro, Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor. The harness is model-agnostic and powered by the open-source Strands Agents SDK. All three features are available at no additional charge.

- **[Cursor 3 Ships Agent-First Interface with Composer 2](https://cursor.com/blog/cursor-3)** - Cursor 3 launched April 2 as the biggest interface overhaul since the product's inception, rebuilt from the ground up around agents rather than files. The release ships with Composer 2—Anysphere's in-house frontier model (based on a Kimi K2.5 base with continued pretraining and large-scale RL)—running at 200+ tokens/second on custom GPU kernels, scoring 61.3 on CursorBench (a 39% improvement over Composer 1.5). Key new capabilities include parallel agent execution with a persistent sidebar, Design Mode for annotating UI directly in a built-in browser, cloud-to-local agent handoff, and a 30+ plugin marketplace. Cursor and Claude Code are now tied at 18% workplace developer adoption per JetBrains' January 2026 survey.

- **[SpaceX Partners with Cursor on Composer 2.5 Training](https://siliconangle.com/2026/04/22/spaceX-partners-cursor-ai-training-floats-potential-60b-acquisition/)** - Business Insider reported in April that Cursor is training its next model, Composer 2.5, on thousands of xAI-supplied GPUs following SpaceX's acquisition of xAI. This marks the first major compute deal between a top AI coding company and xAI's post-SpaceX infrastructure. Composer 2.5 is expected to land in Q2-Q3 2026 with meaningfully better quality than today's Composer 2.

- **[Flat-Rate AI Coding Subscriptions Tightening](https://medium.com/activated-thinker/the-flat-rate-ai-coding-subscription-era-is-ending-what-github-copilot-claude-code-and-cursor-9763e043a63a)** - A broader trend crystallized in April 2026: GitHub reshaped Copilot individual plans and temporarily paused new signups for some tiers; Anthropic shortened Claude Code's server-side prompt cache TTL from 1 hour to 5 minutes, increasing cache rebuild costs in long coding sessions; and Cursor moved frontier models behind Max Mode for legacy plans, accelerating credit burn. Developers note that agentic usage—long multi-step sessions—makes traditional flat-rate pricing uneconomical for providers.
