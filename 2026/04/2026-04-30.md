# AI Daily Digest - 2026-04-30

## Anthropic

- **[Claude Launches Creative Connectors for Adobe, Blender, Ableton & More](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-for-creative-work)** - On April 28, Anthropic released nine new connectors linking Claude directly into professional creative software including Adobe Creative Cloud (50+ tools), Blender, Autodesk Fusion, Ableton, Splice, Affinity, SketchUp, and Resolume. The Adobe connector can orchestrate multi-step workflows across Photoshop, Premiere Pro, Illustrator, and more from a single plain-language description. Anthropic also joined the Blender Development Fund as a patron and is partnering with RISD, Ringling, and Goldsmiths to trial these tools in creative education programs.

- **[Claude Commits to Staying Ad-Free, Expands Free Tier](https://www.anthropic.com/news)** - Anthropic formally pledged that Claude will remain ad-free, contrasting with OpenAI's rollout of ads in ChatGPT for free and Go-tier users. Following the commitment, Anthropic expanded free-tier capabilities to include file creation, connectors, and skills—plus compaction for longer conversations—features previously limited to paid plans.

- **[Claude Opus 4.7 Now Generally Available](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7)** - Released April 16, Claude Opus 4.7 upgrades its predecessor with 3x higher vision resolution (to 2,576 px / ~3.75 MP), a new `xhigh` effort level for finer reasoning/latency control, and task budgets in public beta for agentic runs. It scores 70% on CursorBench (vs. 58% for Opus 4.6) and achieves a +13% lift on Anthropic's internal 93-task coding benchmark. Available across the Claude API, Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud Vertex AI, and Microsoft Foundry at unchanged pricing ($5/$25 per million tokens).

## OpenAI

- **[GPT-5.5 Released: OpenAI's Smartest Model Yet](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-5/)** - OpenAI released GPT-5.5 on April 23, pitching it as its "smartest and most intuitive model yet" with particular strength in agentic coding, computer use, and long-horizon multi-step tasks. It matches GPT-5.4 per-token latency while delivering significantly higher intelligence and using fewer tokens on Codex tasks. Available to Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users in ChatGPT and Codex, with API access as of April 24; GPT-5.5 Pro is also available for Pro/Business/Enterprise plans.

- **[OpenAI + AWS Expand Partnership: GPT-5.5 and Codex on Amazon Bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/top-announcements-of-the-whats-next-with-aws-2026/)** - On April 28, OpenAI and AWS announced an expanded partnership bringing GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.4 to Amazon Bedrock in limited preview, plus Codex on Bedrock and Bedrock Managed Agents powered by OpenAI. This follows an amended Microsoft-OpenAI agreement (April 27) that allows OpenAI to serve products on any cloud provider, ending Microsoft's exclusivity while keeping it as the primary cloud partner through 2032.

- **[ChatGPT Workspace Agents Launch for Teams](https://openai.com/index/introducing-workspace-agents-in-chatgpt/)** - OpenAI introduced Workspace Agents in ChatGPT on April 22, enabling teams to create shared Codex-powered agents that handle repeatable workflows from report preparation to code writing, running in the cloud even when users are offline. Agents integrate with Slack, can run on a schedule, and are available to Business, Enterprise, and Edu plan customers. Separately, the "Codex for (almost) everything" update (April 21) added background computer use, an in-app browser with page commenting, memory, and image generation via gpt-image-1.5; Codex now has 4 million weekly active users.

## Google AI

- **[Deep Research & Deep Research Max Launch, Powered by Gemini 3.1 Pro](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/next-generation-gemini-deep-research/)** - Google DeepMind released two evolutions of its autonomous research agent: a speed-optimized Deep Research and a thoroughness-focused Deep Research Max, both built on Gemini 3.1 Pro. Both support MCP for private data access, native chart/infographic generation, and scored 85.9 on the BrowseComp benchmark (vs. ~60 for the prior Gemini 3 Pro version). Deep Research Max is designed for asynchronous background workflows such as generating overnight due diligence reports.

- **[Google Cloud Posts Record $20B Quarter on Gemini Demand](https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/04/30/google-bets-big-on-ai-across-tv-photos-and-cloud-as-gemini-powers-record-growth/)** - Alphabet reported its strongest Google Cloud quarter ever, surpassing $20 billion in revenue (63% year-over-year growth), with AI solutions led by Gemini Enterprise as the primary growth driver. API token usage hit 16 billion per minute (up from 10B the prior quarter) and Cloud backlog reached $462 billion. Gemini is also expanding to Google TV with Nano Banana image generation, Veo video tools, and Gemini-powered Google Photos search and remixing.

- **[April Gemini Drop: Native Mac App, Personal Intelligence & Music Creation](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-drop-april-2026/)** - The 10th Gemini Drops update added a native macOS desktop app, globally expanding Personal Intelligence (connecting Google apps like Gmail and Calendar for personalized context), NotebookLM organization directly in the app, and new music creation tools. Personal Intelligence is rolling out to international Google AI plan subscribers.

## Developer Tools & Vibe Coding

- **[SpaceX Secures $60B Option to Acquire Cursor; $10B Collaboration Deal Signed](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/21/spacex-says-it-can-buy-cursor-later-this-year-for-60-billion-or-pay-10-billion-for-our-work-together.html)** - In one of the biggest vibe-coding deals ever, SpaceX announced it has given Cursor (Anysphere) a $10 billion collaboration payment and holds an option to fully acquire the company for $60 billion after SpaceX's anticipated IPO this summer. The deal halted Cursor's $2B fundraising round and aims to combine Cursor's Composer model with SpaceX/xAI's Colossus supercomputer to build "the world's best coding and knowledge work AI." Microsoft reportedly also evaluated a Cursor acquisition but withdrew before SpaceX stepped in.

- **[OpenAI Codex Expands with Background Computer Use, In-App Browser & Memory](https://openai.com/index/codex-for-almost-everything/)** - OpenAI's "Codex for (almost) everything" update adds background computer use (multiple agents work in parallel on your Mac without interfering with your own work), an in-app browser with direct page commenting, memory from past sessions, and image generation via gpt-image-1.5. Codex can now schedule future work autonomously, wake up to continue long-running tasks over days or weeks, and integrate with apps like Slack, Gmail, and Notion. The Codex app also shipped on Windows for Business workspaces.

- **[Claude Code Surpasses $2.5B ARR; Quality Issues Fixed & Rate Limits API Launched](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic)** - Claude Code has surpassed $2.5B in annualized revenue and recently resolved three critical quality regressions with v2.1.116 (April 20): reduced default reasoning effort, a caching bug dropping thinking history in extended mode, and an over-verbose prompt that hurt coding quality. Usage limits were reset for affected subscribers. Additionally, a new Rate Limits API launched allowing administrators to programmatically query organization and workspace rate limits, and Managed Agents reached general availability for production-scale long-horizon agent workflows.
