## OpenAI
• OpenAI releases LifeSciBench, a 750-task expert benchmark for AI in real-world life science research — developed with 173 Ph.D.-level scientists from biotech and pharma; tasks span seven workflows including evidence handling, design, and scientific communication; even the top model (GPT-Rosalind) clears only ~1-in-3 tasks, leaving the benchmark far from saturation; signals OpenAI's deepening push into drug discovery R&D — https://openai.com/index/introducing-life-sci-bench/

## Google AI
• Gemini CLI officially ends today (June 18) for Google AI Pro, Ultra, and free users, replaced by Antigravity CLI — the agent-first platform that succeeded it; the open-source repo retains its Apache 2.0 license and stays maintained for enterprise paid-API customers; developer backlash on GitHub and Antigravity CLI issues pages centres on tight usage limits and concerns that open-source community contributions funded a new closed-source successor — https://developers.googleblog.com/an-important-update-transitioning-gemini-cli-to-antigravity-cli/
