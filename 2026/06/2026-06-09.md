# AI Daily Digest — 2026-06-09 🤖

## OpenAI
• OpenAI files confidential S-1 with the SEC and publishes "Built to benefit everyone" AGI manifesto — the second AI lab to begin the IPO process after Anthropic's June 1 filing; Sam Altman and CTO Jakub Pachocki's joint post outlines three missions: build an automated AI researcher, accelerate global economic growth, and give every person on Earth a "personal AGI"; Polymarket assigns 79.5% probability of an IPO by year-end at a $1.5T+ valuation — https://openai.com/index/built-to-benefit-everyone-our-plan/
• OpenAI launches Economic Research Exchange — a new platform for structured, project-based collaborations with external academics studying how AI affects workers, firms, and the broader economy; applications open through July 5, 2026 — https://openai.com/index/economic-research-exchange/
• OpenAI and Dell Technologies partner to deploy Codex on-premises and in hybrid environments — Codex (4M+ weekly users) connects with Dell AI Data Platform and Dell AI Factory to run agent workflows inside enterprise infrastructure; targets regulated industries with strict data-residency requirements and marks Codex's expansion beyond software development into broader knowledge-work automation — https://openai.com/index/dell-codex-enterprise-partnership/

## Meta
• Meta Lab @ Best Buy launches 50+ experiential spaces in US and Canada — 900-square-foot hands-on zones in Best Buy stores let customers try AI glasses and VR headsets in person; Meta cites research showing over 50% of potential buyers want to handle the hardware before purchasing, making in-store presence critical to the AI wearables growth strategy — https://www.pymnts.com/meta/2026/meta-partners-best-buy-to-showcase-ai-and-vr-products/
• Meta opens its ad platform to third-party AI tools — marks a notable shift from Meta's historically closed ad ecosystem, allowing advertisers to bring their own AI optimization software; announcement arrives amid tensions over Chinese authorities ordering Meta to unwind its Manus AI-agent acquisition — https://digiday.com/marketing/meta-opens-its-ad-ecosystem-to-third-party-ai-tools/

## xAI / Grok
• xAI taps Starlink engineer Jack Garabedian to lead Grok's human-data training team — Garabedian, a SpaceX veteran since 2021, takes charge of hundreds of subject-matter experts who train Grok across finance, science, and other domains; the move signals organizational integration between SpaceX and xAI following their merger, per Bloomberg — https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-09/musk-s-xai-taps-starlink-staffer-to-run-grok-training-team

## Industry, Deals & Policy
• UK government launches AI Economics Institute (AIEI) — joint HM Treasury and DSIT body modelled on the AI Safety Institute to study AI's macroeconomic and labour-market effects; launches on the same day as OpenAI's Economic Research Exchange, reflecting coordinated public-private attention to AI's economic disruption — https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-economics-institute-prospectus/ai-economics-institute-aiei-prospectus
• Colorado AI Act enters its final 21-day sprint to June 30 enforcement — the first comprehensive US state high-risk AI law; imposes anti-discrimination, risk-management, and transparency obligations on AI developers and deployers; the White House AI Litigation Task Force has flagged it as a federal-preemption target, creating compliance uncertainty for US companies — https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/
