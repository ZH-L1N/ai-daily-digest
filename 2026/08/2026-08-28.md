## Anthropic

## OpenAI
• Bocconi/OpenAI Economics Research study: ChatGPT access and critical-thinking training produce distinct, complementary benefits — in a 1,000+ student randomized experiment, ChatGPT users scored nearly one full point higher on a 5-point rubric and produced work closer to expert recommendations; a causal-reasoning exercise independently led to more unique ideas; students receiving both showed both effects with no trade-off, challenging assumptions about AI undermining originality — https://openai.com/index/what-students-gain-from-chatgpt-critical-thinking-training/

• Alabama becomes first state to subpoena OpenAI over Hugging Face breach — AG Steve Marshall served OpenAI on Aug 20 (Sept 14 response deadline) investigating possible violations of Alabama's Deceptive Trade Practices Act; 15 other Republican-state AGs had sent a coalition letter demanding document preservation and a halt to relevant testing; the escalation from letter to formal subpoena sets a precedent for state-level AI safety enforcement — https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/24/tech/openai-subpoena-hugging-face-attorney-general-alabama
