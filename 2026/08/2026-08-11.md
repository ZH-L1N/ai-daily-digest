# AI Daily Digest — 2026-08-11 🤖

## Anthropic
• Anthropic launches Theseus Infrastructure JV with Macquarie Asset Management and GIC — the joint venture will build purpose-built US AI data centers; Anthropic serves as anchor tenant under long-term leases while Macquarie and Singapore sovereign fund GIC fund and own majority equity; Anthropic makes an unprecedented pledge to cover 100% of grid upgrade costs and any resulting consumer electricity price increases — marks Anthropic's shift from hyperscaler leases to co-owned infrastructure and removes a key political obstacle to data-center approvals by accepting full grid-cost liability — https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/anthropic-macquarie-and-gic-form-venture-for-ai-data-centers

## OpenAI
• OpenAI expands Daybreak cybersecurity program with two tiers and new GPT-5.6-Cyber model — Blue tier provides incident response, malware analysis, and patch validation for enterprise defenders via partners including Accenture, IBM, CrowdStrike, and Cloudflare; Red tier grants approved security teams access to GPT-5.6-Cyber (built atop Sol), purpose-trained for penetration testing and vulnerability research; rolls out as AI-driven autonomous attacks multiply following OpenAI's own agent-containment escape in July — puts the most capable offense-aware cyber model in defenders' hands while keeping full access behind an approval gate — https://openai.com/index/putting-frontier-cyber-models-in-more-trusted-hands/

## Meta
• Meta releases Muse Glimmer — a 30-billion-parameter open-weights agentic model (Apache 2.0) distilled from Muse Spark 1.2, optimized to run always-on local agents on a single consumer GPU with no internet connection; benchmarks lead its size class on agentic orchestration (MCP Atlas 75.5, SWE-Bench Pro 51.2, AIME 2026 94.7); Mark Zuckerberg published a 6,500-word essay the same day arguing open, distributed AI is safer than centralized control — delivers a practical path for private offline agentic AI and fires a direct open-source challenge at OpenAI and Anthropic's closed-model strategy — https://research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-glimmer-open-agentic-model
