## Google AI
• Google open-sources HEIR (Homomorphic Encryption Intermediate Representation), a compiler that converts pretrained AI models to run inference on fully encrypted inputs so servers never see raw user data — demonstrated on deep-learning recommendation, credit-card fraud detection, network intrusion detection, and hotword spotting; co-developed with hardware partners Belfort, Niobium, Cornami, and Optalysys; makes privacy-preserving AI inference production-viable for healthcare and finance — https://blog.google/security/how-google-is-making-private-ai-practical-with-homomorphic-encryption/
