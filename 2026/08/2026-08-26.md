# AI Daily Digest — 2026-08-26 🤖

## NVIDIA
• NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano 2 announced — entry-level robotics computer delivers 78 TOPS and 8GB of memory in the same compact form factor as its predecessor, with 2× the inference performance of Jetson Orin Nano Super and 40% less power at 15 W; enables LLMs and VLMs including Nemotron, Gemma 4, and Qwen 3 on robots, drones, and vision AI systems for real-time edge reasoning; partners Cognex, Doosan Bobcat, Matic, and Wing are early adopters; module and developer kit expected in H1 2027 — https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-jetson-orin-nano-2-robotics-computer-to-redefine-entry-level-edge-ai

• NVIDIA Q2 FY27 earnings due after market close today — company guided $91B revenue (±2%); Wall Street consensus sits at ~$92B, nearly doubling Q2 FY26's $46.7B; Data Center revenue expected near $85B driven by Blackwell and Vera Rubin ramps; key watch items are Q3 guide (analysts project $100B+), gross margin trajectory, and any updated China H20 commentary — https://investor.nvidia.com/events-and-presentations/events-and-presentations/event-details/2026/NVIDIA-2nd-Quarter-FY27-Financial-Results/default.aspx

• NVIDIA pays $6B to license Poolside's Model Factory — plus a $1B equity investment at a $12B valuation and job offers to 109 Poolside engineers; the deal bolsters NVIDIA's Nemotron open-weight model family and follows Poolside losing access to a 40,000-GPU cluster after failing to close a $2B fundraise in late 2025; NVIDIA enters the model layer directly to compete with DeepSeek and frontier labs while using freely available models to drive broader chip demand — https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/nvidia-to-pay-ai-startup-poolside-a-6-billion-license-newcomer-says

## OpenAI
• ChatGPT desktop app launches in public preview on Linux — available on Ubuntu 24.04 LTS and 26.04 LTS, Debian 13, and Fedora 43 and 44; supports the built-in browser and browser actions but not yet native desktop app control; the release also adds interactive quizzes across all consumer and Edu plans and the ability to change a project's memory setting post-creation — broadens ChatGPT's reach to the Linux developer user base — https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes

• DALL·E GPT retiring from ChatGPT on August 30 — the official image-generation GPT sunset; users urged to download saved images before then; all image creation consolidates to ChatGPT Images; user-created GPTs with image generation remain unaffected — https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes
