# AI Daily Digest — 2026-08-13 🤖

## OpenAI
• ChatGPT ads expand to UK, Mexico, Brazil, Japan, and South Korea — OpenAI extended its in-chat advertising program to five new international markets on Aug 11, building on the February US launch; sponsored placements appear below responses for Free and Go users, are clearly labeled, do not influence answers, and remain absent on paid plans; advertisers receive only aggregate campaign metrics and never see user conversations — signals OpenAI's intent to monetize its global free-tier base at scale and cements ads as a permanent ChatGPT feature, intensifying the ad-versus-assistant trust debate — https://openai.com/index/testing-ads-in-chatgpt/
• OpenAI expands Daybreak cybersecurity initiative with GPT-5.6-Cyber model, now on AWS Bedrock — OpenAI introduced GPT-5.6-Cyber for advanced authorized security work alongside two program tiers: Daybreak Blue (defensive: vulnerability research, code review, incident response) and Daybreak Red (offensive emulation for approved red-teaming); the full Daybreak model family also landed on AWS Bedrock on Aug 11 — puts frontier AI directly in enterprise security teams' cloud-native workflows, competing with Google's security-focused Gemini deployments — https://openai.com/index/expanding-daybreak-as-the-cyber-defense-window-narrows/

## Google AI
• Gemini app surpasses 1 billion monthly active users, Google's fastest-growing product ever — CEO Sundar Pichai announced on Aug 11 that Gemini is Google's 14th billion-user product, adding roughly 50 million users in the three weeks since the July 22 earnings call; 63% of users now speak directly to Gemini, 1-in-5 Live sessions use live camera or screen sharing, the app generates 150M+ images per day, and 100M+ users are on iOS — places Gemini at parity with ChatGPT's comparable June 2026 milestone, validating Google's ambient AI-first strategy at consumer scale — https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/one-billion-monthly-users/

## Meta
• Muse Glimmer open-weights launched; Muse Spark 1.2 weights to follow; Zuckerberg publishes 6,500-word open-source AI manifesto — Meta released Muse Glimmer, a permissively licensed on-device model family designed to run on a laptop, and committed to open-sourcing its frontier Muse Spark 1.2 weights; Zuckerberg's essay frames open-source US AI as the strategic counterweight to Chinese open-weight models (DeepSeek, Moonshot) and urges Washington to reduce training-data friction disadvantaging American labs; Muse Code, a parallel-sub-agent coding assistant built on Muse Spark 1.2 at $1.25/$4.25 per million tokens, launched in beta alongside — first time Meta opens weights on its most advanced model; positions open strategy as both a policy argument and an enterprise moat against closed-source rivals — https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/10/meta-muse-glimmer-open-weight-ai.html

## xAI / Grok
