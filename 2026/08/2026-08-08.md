# AI Daily Digest — 2026-08-08 🤖

## OpenAI
• Codex ships portable Agent Plugins — searchable across local, personal, workspace, and remote catalogs — along with persistent conversation sections, an --approve-for-me CLI flag for automated approvals, and opt-in MCP 2026-07-28 protocol support (paginated discovery, multi-round requests) — significantly expands Codex's integration surface for enterprise agentic workflows and reduces approval friction in CI/CD pipelines — https://developers.openai.com/codex/changelog

## Google AI

## NVIDIA
• NVIDIA leads Open Secure AI Alliance (now 120+ members including Amazon, Cisco, CrowdStrike, and Microsoft) in unveiling SAFE guidelines for agentic AI cybersecurity at Black Hat USA 2026; simultaneously open-sources NOOA (NVIDIA Object-Oriented Agents, Apache 2.0), a Python framework that models agent behavior as classes to make it testable, traceable, and auditable — direct response to the growing wave of agentic AI breaches in enterprise production systems — https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/open-secure-ai-alliance-contributions/

## Meta
• Meta enters the coding-agent market with Muse Code, a terminal-based agent (macOS/Linux beta) powered by the new coding-optimized Muse Spark 1.2 model — handles full software engineering tasks (plan, write, validate, resume-on-crash) across large codebases using persistent background agents; a contributor tier at $0.10/$0.20 per million tokens undercuts Claude Code and OpenAI Codex on price by more than 10× — https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/05/meta-launches-muse-code-an-ai-agent-for-large-code-bases/

## xAI / Grok
• Tesla and SpaceX confirm Terafab will be built in Grimes County, TX with a $16.8B first-phase investment — the vertically integrated AI chip fab (logic, memory, packaging, and testing under one roof, 100M+ sq ft) will produce chips for Optimus robots, Cybercab robotaxis, and SpaceX orbital data centers, with Intel as manufacturing partner; total multi-phase cost could reach $119B — https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/06/tesla-and-spacex-will-invest-16-8b-to-start-building-terafab-chip-factory-in-texas/

## Hardware & Chips
• AMD acquires Taalas (Toronto), the startup that etches AI model weights directly into silicon rather than loading them from DRAM — HC1 chip demonstrated 16,960 tokens/sec on Llama 3.1 8B (~48× faster than NVIDIA GPUs at roughly one-tenth the power draw); AMD plans to integrate Taalas silicon alongside Instinct GPUs and EPYC CPUs in its Helios rack-scale systems; deal expected to close Q4 2026, terms undisclosed — https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1296/amd-acquires-taalas-to-advance-compute-solutions-for-rapidly-growing-ai-inference-market
