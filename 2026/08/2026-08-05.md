## NVIDIA
• Alpamayo 2 Super open autonomous-vehicle model released for commercial use — built on Cosmos 3 Super Reasoner and RL post-training, it ranks first on LingoQA among ~40 models (+17 pts vs. Qwen2.5-VL 72B, +23 pts vs. GPT-4o); produces five tightly coupled outputs per driving scenario (trajectory, chain-of-causation reasoning traces, meta-action, auto-labels, and visual QA with 2D grounding); available under the permissive OpenMDW-1.1 license on Hugging Face, enabling a cloud-to-car workflow for robotaxi fleets — https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/alpamayo-2-super-open-model-now-available/

## Hardware & Chips
• SK hynix and Sandisk publish the first open High Bandwidth Flash specification via the Open Compute Project at FMS 2026 — defines a new memory tier between HBM and SSDs: up to 512 GB per device (8-high and 16-high NAND stacks), three bandwidth grades from 0.4 to 3.0 TB/s, and the UCIe chiplet interconnect for direct GPU/CPU attachment; Google and Tenstorrent joined the consortium; targets the capacity-cost bottleneck in large-scale AI inference — https://news.skhynix.com/en/hbf-at-fms-2026-01/
