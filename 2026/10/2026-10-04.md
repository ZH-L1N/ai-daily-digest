## Anthropic

## Meta
• Meta parts ways with Virtue AI safety team four months after acqui-hire — all three Virtue AI co-founders (Bo Li, Dawn Song, and Sanmi Koyejo) plus other staff brought into Meta Superintelligence Labs in June 2026 are departing; Meta spokesperson cited "clashing work styles" and said the arrangement "didn't work out as planned"; the team had AI safety credentials spanning Anthropic, OpenAI, and NIST and was hired to bolster Meta's safety credibility ahead of regulation; exit comes as Meta scales Muse aggressively and faces growing scrutiny over AI agent incidents — https://www.semafor.com/article/10/02/2026/meta-parts-ways-with-virtue-ai

## Google AI
• Gemini tightens model access tiers from October 9 — free users lose Flash and Pro access, restricted to Flash-Lite only; AI Plus ($4.99/mo) also loses Pro; AI Pro ($19.99/mo) gains Deep Think, previously limited to $99.99+ plans; Gemini 4 Argon begins exclusive rollout to AI Ultra subscribers with broader tier classification still TBD; marks Google's pivot from user-acquisition to active monetization after a year of expansive free model access — https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/digital/google-gemini-model-access-changes-on-october-9-what-free-and-paid-users-need-to-know-111951.htm

## Hardware & Chips
• Apple tightens macOS Full Disk Access controls to contain AI agent data risks — days after a journalist reported Meta's Muse app read private messages without clear user awareness, Apple warned developers that "some are exposing everything on users' systems—including files, mail, messages, and even browsing history—without users' full knowledge" and announced forthcoming controls requiring "very explicit user action" to grant the sweeping permission; directly impacts desktop agents like Muse, Dots, Claude, and Grok Bot that currently run with broad macOS access; no implementation date specified — https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/02/apple-says-its-tightening-macos-full-disk-access-controls-due-to-new-risks-from-ai-agents/

## Industry, Deals & Policy
• Trump convenes White House "Joint Commitment on Frontier Responsibilities" with six frontier labs — voluntary accord signed Sept 29 by OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, xAI, and NVIDIA pledges four layers of self-oversight: internal monitoring controls, independent external audits, board-level safety committees, and regular standards meetings; Trump called it "morally binding" but the pact carries no legal penalties or enforcement mechanism; critics call it "security theater"; major labs are separately advancing an industry-run AI safety standards body planned for late 2026 or early 2027 — https://www.axios.com/2026/09/29/trump-ai-voluntary-safety-white-house-zuckerberg
