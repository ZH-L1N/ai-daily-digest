## NVIDIA

## Open-source models & agents
• Sakana AI releases Fugu Max and Fugu Ultra v2 (September 11) — dual-tier update to its learned orchestration system, which routes tasks across a pool of open-weight and specialist models behind one OpenAI-compatible API; Fugu Max targets cost efficiency at $2/$6 per million input/output tokens (40–60% cheaper than Sonnet 5 at output) and leads six benchmarks; Fugu Ultra v2 targets peak capability, topping seven of eight benchmarks including 74.3 on DeepSWE and 48.3 on Chartography — all without Fable 5.1 or GPT-6 Astra in its model pool, making the vendor-independence claim verifiable — demonstrates orchestrated open-model ensembles can match frontier single-model quality at a fraction of the cost — https://sakana.ai/fugu-max-release/

## Developer Tools & Vibe Coding
• Cognition ships Devin Fusion in Devin Desktop and CLI (September 12) — pairs a frontier "lead" model (Fable 5.1 or GPT-6 Astra recommended) with SWE-2 as a cheaper sidekick executor; independent benchmarks by Artificial Analysis and Vals AI confirm 36–39% cost reduction while maintaining frontier coding quality; the counterintuitive finding: using Fable 5.1 as lead (2× per-token price) ends up cheaper per task than Opus 4.8 because Fable delegates earlier and more precisely, eliminating sidekick rework — makes agentic coding at frontier quality materially more affordable for Devin production users — https://cognition.com/blog/local-fusion
