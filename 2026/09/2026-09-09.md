## Anthropic
• Claude EU AI Act watermark detection API enters private preview — Anthropic rolls out a query endpoint for entities designated under EU law (regulators, law enforcement, media, fact-checkers, independent researchers) to test whether text was likely generated by Claude models released after August 2, 2026; the watermark carries no user or content data and does not affect output quality; fulfills Anthropic's obligations under the EU AI Act's Code of Practice on AI-Generated Content Transparency, signed in July alongside 190 other AI providers — https://anthropic.com/news

## Google AI
• AlphaGenome Atlas debuts — freely searchable, 1-petabyte database pre-computing the predicted molecular impact of all 9 billion possible single-letter DNA variants in the human genome; the companion AlphaGenome Variant Impact (AVI) score more than doubles researchers' ability to identify rare-disease-causing mutations versus prior state-of-the-art tools; free for academic use via web portal and API, with commercial access via Google Cloud coming soon — https://deepmind.google/blog/alphagenome-atlas-a-predictive-map-of-every-possible-dna-letter-change-in-the-human-genome/
