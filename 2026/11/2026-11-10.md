## Google AI
• Google DeepMind's AlphaProof Code formally verifies Signal's cryptographic protocol — Gemini Carbon agents generated over 6 million lines of Lean proof in just 4 weeks, a task that historically takes expert teams years; first time any method (AI or otherwise) has verified a live deployed cryptographic protocol at this implementation depth; proves Signal's key security properties down to compiled machine code; agents identified only 1% of the codebase requiring human review; artifacts open-sourced — demonstrates agentic AI as a scalable tool for elite software security assurance — https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-alphaproof-code/
