## Anthropic
• METR independent audit finds Anthropic's AI R&D pace has roughly doubled — after an 11-week high-access review inside Anthropic (Aug 12–Oct 29), independent evaluator METR concluded that as of October 2026 Anthropic is improving its models on well-specified tasks at approximately 2× their March 2024–October 2025 rate; the Anthropic Epoch Capabilities Index (AECI) grew 1.9× faster since October 2025 (95% CI: 1.5–2.8×); METR attributes roughly 80% of the speedup to AI assisting Anthropic's own research, with no compute, data, or algorithmic bottleneck visible over the next 12 months; Anthropic redacted most of the evidence METR deemed most informative, citing competitive sensitivity — the first independent insider assessment to quantify AI-on-AI acceleration at a frontier lab, arriving just as Anthropic prepares its Nasdaq IPO — https://metr.org/blog/2026-11-05-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessment/
