# AI Daily Digest - 2026-05-13

## Anthropic

- **[Claude for Legal Formally Launches with 12 Practice-Area Plugins](https://www.artificiallawyer.com/2026/05/12/claude-for-legal-launches-may-reshape-the-legal-tech-world/)** - Anthropic formally launched Claude for Legal, a dedicated solution for law firms and in-house legal teams, featuring twelve new practice-area plugins (Commercial, Employment, Privacy, Product, Corporate, AI Governance) and integrations with Thomson Reuters CoCounsel, Harvey, Legora, and the Free Law Project. Claude Opus 4.7 scored 90.9% on BigLaw Bench — the highest of any Claude model — and partners describe Claude as standout for grounding and citation faithfulness. The launch signals Anthropic making legal one of its highest-priority vertical markets.

- **[Claude Code Ships Agent View, /goal Command & Richer Plugin Tools](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude-code)** - Claude Code's latest release (first seen May 11) adds an agent view — a single list of every running, blocked, or completed session, accessible via `claude agents` — and a new `/goal` command that lets users set a completion condition and have Claude keep working across turns until it is met. The update also adds `claude plugin details <name>` for per-plugin token-cost inspection, a `/scroll-speed` tuning command, and broad reliability fixes across login, MCP, Remote Control, diffs, and editor integrations.

- **[Claude for Microsoft 365 Add-ins Goes Generally Available](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude)** - Claude add-ins for Excel, PowerPoint, and Word are now generally available for paid Claude plans, with Claude for Outlook entering public beta. The integration keeps conversation context across Office apps, syncs edits between open files, and brings inbox triage and draft-reply capabilities to Outlook. This extends Anthropic's enterprise productivity push alongside its AWS and SAP platform deals.

- **[SAP and Anthropic Partner to Embed Claude Across SAP Business AI Platform](https://news.sap.com/2026/05/sap-anthropic-to-bring-claude-sap-business-ai-platform/)** - SAP and Anthropic announced a deep partnership to embed Claude across SAP's AI-enabled solution portfolio — including SAP S/4HANA, SuccessFactors, and Ariba — powered by Joule and Joule agents via MCP. Claude will handle tasks such as closing the quarter, answering complex HR queries, and rerouting supplier orders in real time. The deal was announced at SAP Sapphire 2026 and positions Claude as a core agentic layer for hundreds of thousands of SAP enterprise customers.

## OpenAI

- **[OpenAI Launches Daybreak: AI-Powered Cyber Defense Platform](https://openai.com/daybreak/)** - OpenAI unveiled Daybreak, a cybersecurity initiative combining GPT-5.5, Codex Security, and a partner ecosystem (Cisco, Cloudflare, CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks, Zscaler, and others) to help organizations find and patch vulnerabilities continuously across the software development lifecycle. The platform ships three access tiers — standard GPT-5.5, GPT-5.5 with Trusted Access for Cyber for defensive work, and the permissive GPT-5.5-Cyber for vetted red-teaming — and builds editable threat models from a company's own repo to surface exploitable attack paths. Daybreak directly answers Anthropic's Project Glasswing / Claude Mythos initiative and marks a strategic pivot from productivity tool to operational cybersecurity infrastructure.

- **[OpenAI Deployment Company (DeployCo) Open for Business](https://openai.com/index/openai-launches-the-deployment-company/)** - OpenAI's new enterprise deployment subsidiary — backed by TPG, Goldman Sachs, SoftBank, and others at a $4B+ valuation — is now embedding Forward Deployed Engineers into organizations to move AI from pilot to production. The company acquired UK-based Tomoro to seed the FDE team with ~150 experienced engineers from day one. DeployCo is explicitly modeled on Palantir's playbook for turning frontier AI into durable enterprise systems.

## Google AI

- **[Android Show: I/O Edition Delivers Googlebook Laptops, Vibe-Coded Widgets & Agentic Gemini](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/12/everything-google-announced-at-its-android-show-from-googlebooks-to-vibe-coded-widgets/)** - At its virtual pre-I/O showcase on May 12, Google announced Googlebook — a new laptop line built from the ground up for Gemini Intelligence, launching this fall with partners Acer, Asus, Dell, HP, and Lenovo. Android also gains a "Create My Widget" feature for vibe-coding custom home-screen widgets via natural language (first on Samsung Galaxy and Pixel this summer), Gemini in Chrome on Android with experimental auto-browse, and expanded Gemini Intelligence that performs multi-step agentic tasks across apps — pulling grocery lists into Instacart carts or finding events on Expedia from a photo. Google I/O proper begins May 19.

- **[Google DeepMind Introduces Gemini-Powered AI Mouse Pointer](https://deepmind.google/blog/ai-pointer/)** - DeepMind published an experimental AI-enabled pointer powered by Gemini that captures visual and semantic context around the cursor, eliminating the need for users to manually write prompts for on-screen content. Two live demos are available in Google AI Studio (image editing and map search by pointing and speaking), with a "Magic Pointer" integration rolling out in Chrome today and Googlebook laptops next. The system is built on four principles — Maintain the flow, Show and tell, Embrace the power of "This" and "That", and Turn pixels into actionable entities — designed to shift context-providing work from user to computer.

## Developer Tools & Vibe Coding

- **[Cursor SDK Hits Public Beta: Coding Agents as Deployable Infrastructure](https://cursor.com/blog/typescript-sdk)** - Cursor released the Cursor SDK (`npm install @cursor/sdk`) in public beta, exposing the same agent runtime, codebase indexing, MCP server support, skills, hooks, and subagents that power the Cursor desktop app and CLI as a TypeScript API. Teams can now invoke Cursor agents programmatically from CI/CD pipelines, backend services, or embedded products — running locally, on Cursor's sandboxed cloud VMs, or on self-hosted workers. Token-based pricing (Composer 2 at $0.50/M input) aligns costs with usage; companies like Rippling, Notion, and Faire are already running it in production.

- **[GitHub Copilot Switches to Usage-Based Billing on June 1, 2026](https://fungies.io/best-ai-coding-tools-2026-comparison/)** - Starting June 1, GitHub Copilot is moving from request-based to token consumption billing via GitHub AI Credits, meaning light users may see lower bills while heavy agent-mode users could pay more. The shift comes as every major AI coding tool races to add agentic features, and Copilot continues to expand Claude and Codex model access to all paid tiers. GitHub Copilot retains roughly 42% market share and the broadest IDE support, making the pricing model change a potentially significant cost signal for enterprise engineering teams.
