# AI Daily Digest - 2026-05-17

## Anthropic

- **[Claude Pushes Deeper into Legal Work with 20+ MCP Connectors and 12 Practice-Area Plugins](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/legal-ops-and-tech/anthropic-pushes-deeper-into-legal-work-with-claude-updates)** - On May 14, Anthropic unveiled 12 new AI plugins for specific legal practice areas including corporate, regulatory, employment, and M&A law, alongside 20+ MCP connectors linking Claude to outside legal tech software. The update builds on Claude Cowork's existing contract redlining tool and marks what one legal technology consultant called "a major transition for Claude from backroom to front room" in the legal industry. Legal professionals have become the most engaged Claude Cowork users of any knowledge-work function since the February launch, and a preceding Claude for Legal webinar drew 20,000 sign-ups.

- **[Fast Mode Now Available for Claude Opus 4.7 on Developer Platform](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic)** - The Claude Developer Platform added Fast mode support for Claude Opus 4.7, bringing significantly faster output token generation in research preview. Developers can activate it by setting `speed: "fast"` with `model: "claude-opus-4-7"` and the `fast-mode-2026-02-01` beta header; pricing and rate limits remain identical to Opus 4.6 fast mode. Interested customers are directed to join a waitlist to access the feature.

- **[Claude Code Broad Update: Agent View, New Hook Types, Improved MCP Handling](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude)** - A recent Claude Code release ships a wide-ranging set of improvements: a new agent view for managing multiple CLI sessions from one place (start, background, peek at status, jump back in), a `terminalSequence` field in hook JSON output for desktop notifications and window title updates, new `CLAUDE_CODE_PLUGIN_PREFER_HTTPS` and `ANTHROPIC_WORKSPACE_ID` environment variables, and a `/feedback` command that can now include sessions from the last 24 hours or 7 days. Additional fixes cover prompts, UI, voice, Windows, and MCP server reliability.

## OpenAI

- **[ChatGPT Improves Recognition of Risk Escalation Across Sensitive Conversations](https://openai.com/index/helping-chatgpt-better-recognize-context-in-sensitive-conversations/)** - Released May 14, ChatGPT received updates to better detect emerging suicide, self-harm, and harm-to-others risk across the full arc of a conversation — not just within individual messages. New safety summaries persist across chat sessions, improving the model's ability to de-escalate, refuse harmful details, and respond more carefully in high-risk scenarios. OpenAI says this is its first systematic work on cross-turn safety context and may be extended to other high-risk areas like biology or cybersecurity.

- **[Codex Gets a Secure Sandbox on Windows](https://openai.com/index/building-a-safe-effective-sandbox-to-enable-codex-on-windows/)** - OpenAI published an engineering deep-dive (May 14) on building proper security sandboxing for Codex on Windows. Previously, Windows users had to choose between approving nearly every command or enabling unrestricted "Full Access" mode; the new sandbox introduces elevated binary design, dedicated setup and runner binaries, firewall-backed network blocking, and tighter file-write controls. The effort addresses a core safety gap in enterprise deployments where sensitive credentials and security policies must be protected.

- **[New Voice Intelligence Models Released in the API](https://openai.com/index/advancing-voice-intelligence-with-new-models-in-the-api/)** - Also on May 14, OpenAI released updated voice intelligence models in its API, improving real-time audio capabilities for developers building voice-first applications. The release expands the audio model lineup available to API users and continues OpenAI's push to make voice an equal first-class modality alongside text and code.

## Google AI

- **[Google I/O 2026 Keynote Is Tomorrow (May 19): Gemini 4, Android XR Glasses, and Aluminium OS Expected](https://io.google/2026/)** - Google I/O 2026 opens tomorrow at 10 AM PT, with the keynote widely expected to reveal a next-generation Gemini model (potentially Gemini 4.0, described as a major overhaul with faster responses, improved reasoning, and unified multimodal architecture), the debut of Android XR smart glasses (confirmed by Google), and the first detailed look at Aluminium OS — Google's Android-based laptop operating system. The developer keynote follows at 1:30 PM PT. All sessions stream live at io.google. The Android Show I/O Edition on May 12 pre-empted most Android 17 and Googlebook announcements, leaving the main keynote free to focus on AI platform strategy, agentic features, and hardware partnerships.

- **[Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite Reaches General Availability on Google Cloud](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/release-notes)** - Google's most cost-efficient Gemini model, 3.1 Flash-Lite, has graduated from preview to general availability on the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform (formerly Vertex AI). Provisioned Throughput support for Flash-Lite is also now available, offering predictable performance SLOs for production workloads. Gemini 3.1 Pro and 3 Flash are also now accessible in Gemini Enterprise in Limited Availability, giving enterprise users production-grade service level objectives for these models as the preview period continues.

- **[Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite Distillation Service Enters Early Access](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/release-notes)** - Alongside the Flash-Lite GA, Google introduced a Gemini Distillation Service in Early Access, allowing enterprises to fine-tune smaller, custom models using knowledge distilled from larger Gemini models. The service is part of Google's broader push to make enterprise-grade Gemini performance available at lower inference cost and latency.

## Developer Tools & Vibe Coding

- **[Code w/ Claude Developer Conference Heads to London (May 20–21) and Tokyo (June 5–6)](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic)** - Following the San Francisco kickoff on May 6, Anthropic is taking its "Code w/ Claude" developer conference to London next week (May 20–21) and to Tokyo in June (June 5–6). All Day 1 keynotes and breakout sessions will be streamed live. Sessions go behind the scenes of building Claude with Anthropic teams and feature customers including Asana, Cursor, GitHub, Replit, and Vercel discussing production-ready agent design and agentic development patterns.

- **[xAI Launches Grok Build: Coding Agent with Parallel Agents and Arena Mode](https://devops.com/xai-enters-the-coding-agent-race-with-grok-build/)** - xAI has launched Grok Build, its first coding agent, currently in early testing for paying subscribers. The agent can run up to eight parallel AI agents simultaneously through a plan-search-build workflow. Its standout feature, "Arena Mode," automatically scores and ranks competing outputs before any developer reviews them — a novel approach to AI-native code evaluation. Grok Build enters a market where Claude Code and Codex CLI hold significant head starts; its 256K context window currently trails Claude Opus 4.7 and GPT-5.4, both of which offer 1M+ token contexts.

- **[SpaceX/Cursor $60B Acquisition Option: Awaiting SpaceX's Summer IPO](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/21/spacex-says-it-can-buy-cursor-later-this-year-for-60-billion-or-pay-10-billion-for-our-work-together.html)** - SpaceX (merged with xAI) struck a deal with AI coding startup Cursor in late April, securing the right to acquire the company for $60 billion after its planned summer IPO, or alternatively pay $10 billion for collaborative AI development using the Colossus supercomputer. Cursor — with annualized revenue now exceeding $2 billion and 64% of Fortune 500 companies among its users — halted a planned $2 billion fundraise in favor of the deal. The acquisition is on hold until after SpaceX's IPO to avoid updating confidential financial filings. If completed, it would be the largest deal in the AI developer tools space and give xAI an immediate foothold against Claude Code and Codex.
