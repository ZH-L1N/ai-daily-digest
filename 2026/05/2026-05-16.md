# AI Daily Digest - 2026-05-16

## Anthropic

- **[Claude for Small Business Launches with SMB Tool Integrations](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-for-small-business)** - Anthropic launched Claude for Small Business on May 13, a package of connectors and ready-to-run workflows that put Claude inside QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot, Canva, DocuSign, Google Workspace, and Microsoft 365. The toggle-install product covers tasks including payroll planning, month-end close, cash-flow forecasting, and campaign creation. Anthropic is pairing the launch with a free 10-stop U.S. workshop tour and an on-demand AI training course co-developed with PayPal; attendees receive a one-month Claude Max subscription.

- **[PwC Expands Strategic Alliance with Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/pwc-expanded-partnership)** - Anthropic and PwC announced an expansion of their strategic alliance on May 14, deepening how PwC uses Claude to build technology, execute deals, and reinvent enterprise functions for clients. PwC will roll out Claude Code and Claude Cowork starting with U.S. teams and expanding toward a global workforce of hundreds of thousands; the companies will jointly train and certify 30,000 PwC professionals on Claude and establish a Center of Excellence. Production deployments across financial services, pharma, insurance underwriting, and HR transformation are already reporting delivery-time improvements of up to 70%.

- **[Anthropic Forms $200 Million Partnership with Gates Foundation](https://www.anthropic.com/news/gates-foundation-partnership)** - Announced May 14, Anthropic is committing $200 million in grant funding, Claude usage credits, and technical support for programs in global health, life sciences, education, and economic mobility over the next four years. The partnership includes building healthcare-intelligence connectors, evaluation frameworks, and public-good benchmarks for AI tools in math tutoring, college advising, and K-12 tutoring.

- **[Claude Code Ships May 15 Update: Plugin Dependency Enforcement, Opus 4.7 Fast Mode](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/changelog)** - The May 15 Claude Code release adds plugin dependency enforcement (preventing orphaned plugin disables), projected context cost estimates in the plugin marketplace, and a new `worktree.bgIsolation` setting. Notably, fast mode now defaults to Opus 4.7 (upgraded from Opus 4.6), and PowerShell tool now passes `-ExecutionPolicy Bypass` by default. Numerous stability fixes land for Windows, macOS, background sessions, and MCP server reliability.

## OpenAI

- **[Codex Comes to ChatGPT Mobile App on iOS and Android](https://openai.com/index/work-with-codex-from-anywhere/)** - OpenAI launched Codex in preview inside the ChatGPT mobile app on May 15, available across all plans including Free and Go. The phone acts as a secure control surface for Codex sessions running on a connected laptop, Mac mini, devbox, or remote environment — files, credentials, and permissions stay on the host machine. Developers can start work, review outputs, approve commands, and follow terminal output and diffs in real time from their phone; over 4 million people now use Codex every week. Windows phone support is listed as coming soon.

- **[ChatGPT Launches Personal Finance Tools with Plaid Integration](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-personal-finance-will-let-you-connect-bank-accounts/)** - On May 15, OpenAI launched a preview of personal finance tools for ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the U.S., with Plaid-powered connections to over 12,000 financial institutions including Schwab, Fidelity, Chase, Robinhood, American Express, and Capital One. Users get a dashboard of portfolio performance, spending, subscriptions, and upcoming payments, with planned support for Intuit to enable tax-impact analysis and credit-card approval predictions. OpenAI notes that more than 200 million users already ask financial questions to ChatGPT monthly, and the new GPT-5.5 model's stronger contextual reasoning is central to the feature.

- **[OpenAI Launches the Deployment Company](https://openai.com/index/openai-launches-the-deployment-company/)** - OpenAI announced on May 11 the creation of the OpenAI Deployment Company, a new entity designed to embed Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) directly into organizations working on complex AI projects. The company simultaneously agreed to acquire Tomoro to seed the venture with experienced FDEs from day one. The focus is on redesigning organizational infrastructure and critical workflows around AI, targeting large enterprises with demanding operational environments.

## Google AI

- **[Gemini Intelligence Brings Agentic AI to Android, Googlebooks Laptops Announced](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/12/google-races-put-gemini-at-center-of-android-before-apples-ai-reboot.html)** - Unveiled at The Android Show I/O Edition on May 12, Gemini Intelligence is Google's agentic push that lets Gemini move across apps, understand what's on screen, and complete multi-step tasks — from building shopping carts to booking reservations — without users jumping between services. Google also announced "Googlebooks," a new category of premium Android-powered laptops from Acer, ASUS, and Lenovo due this fall, with Gemini baked in, Magic Pointer, and AI-generated widgets. Android Auto is also being redesigned around Gemini, including what Google calls its biggest Maps update in a decade.

- **[Google I/O 2026 Keynote Set for May 19 — New Gemini Model and Android XR Glasses Expected](https://www.androidauthority.com/what-to-expect-from-google-io-2026-3664979/)** - Google I/O 2026 kicks off May 19 at 10 AM PT, with the keynote expected to center heavily on AI: a new Gemini model (possibly Gemini 3.2, 3.5, or 4.0), agentic coding updates, Android 17, and a preview of Android XR smart glasses. Google has already confirmed Android XR glasses will be shown, with partnerships spanning Warby Parker, Gentle Monster, and XREAL. Aluminium OS — Google's Android-based laptop OS — is also expected to feature, with a 2026 launch previously confirmed by Android ecosystem president Sameer Samat.

- **[Gemini 3 Deep Think Updated for Science and Research](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-deep-think/)** - Google released a major upgrade to Gemini 3 Deep Think, now available in the Gemini app for Google AI Ultra subscribers and in early access via the Gemini API for researchers and enterprises. The updated mode demonstrated gold medal-level results on the written sections of the 2025 International Physics and Chemistry Olympiads, and successfully identified a subtle logical flaw in a mathematics paper that had previously passed human peer review. Deep Think can now also turn a sketch into a 3D-printable object by modeling the shape and generating a printable file.

## Developer Tools & Vibe Coding

- **[Microsoft Cancels Claude Code Licenses, Redirects Engineers to GitHub Copilot CLI](https://www.windowscentral.com/microsoft/microsoft-cancels-claude-code-licenses-shifting-developers-to-github-copilot-cli-a-move-likely-driven-by-financial-motives)** - Reported May 15, Microsoft is canceling the Claude Code licenses it had opened to employees (including developers, PMs, and designers) in December 2025, steering them back toward GitHub Copilot CLI. The move is widely seen as financially motivated, with Microsoft preferring to push its own Copilot product over paying Anthropic for enterprise Claude Code seats.

- **[Anthropic Extends 50% Claude Code Usage Limit Boost Through July 13](https://peerlist.io/cnyouzige/articles/ai-agents-news--may-15-2026-claude-code-expansion-microsoft-)** - Anthropic announced a temporary 50% increase in weekly Claude Code usage limits through July 13, 2026, stacking on top of the previously doubled 5-hour limit. The expansion is designed to reduce experimentation costs and encourage deeper integration into Anthropic's API ecosystem, directly competing with OpenAI's offer of two months of free Codex access to enterprises migrating from Claude Code.

- **[GitHub Copilot CLI v1.0.48 Ships, Usage-Based Billing Transition on Track for June 1](https://www.havoptic.com/tools/github-copilot)** - GitHub Copilot CLI v1.0.48 (released May 14) updates the model picker to display actual token prices instead of dot indicators for usage-based billing users. This continues the countdown to GitHub Copilot's full transition to token-based AI Credits billing on June 1, 2026 — retiring "Premium requests" in favor of metered credit consumption, mirroring shifts already made by Cursor (June 2025) and Windsurf (March 2026). GitHub has also tightened individual plan limits and removed Opus models from the base Pro tier.

- **[Notion Adds Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor Agent Integrations](https://peerlist.io/cnyouzige/articles/ai-agents-news--may-15-2026-claude-code-expansion-microsoft-)** - Notion announced a major developer-platform expansion that, among other updates, lets users communicate directly with external AI agents inside Notion itself. Current integrations include Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, and Decagon, reflecting a broader shift where productivity tools are evolving into orchestration layers for AI agents and workflows.
