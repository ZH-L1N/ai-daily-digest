# AI Daily Digest - 2026-05-15

## Anthropic

- **[PwC Deploys Claude to Build Tech, Execute Deals, and Reinvent Enterprise Functions](https://www.anthropic.com/news/pwc-expanded-partnership)** - Anthropic and PwC expanded their strategic alliance, with PwC rolling out Claude Code and Cowork to hundreds of thousands of professionals globally and establishing a joint Center of Excellence to train and certify 30,000 US professionals on Claude. Live production deployments are already delivering up to 70% faster delivery times — insurance underwriting compressed from 10 weeks to 10 days, incident response from hours to minutes — and PwC is launching a new Office of the CFO business group built entirely around Anthropic's technology stack.

- **[Anthropic Forms $200 Million Partnership with the Gates Foundation](https://www.anthropic.com/news/gates-foundation-partnership)** - Anthropic committed $200 million in grant funding, Claude credits, and technical support to the Gates Foundation over four years, covering global health (vaccine screening for polio and HPV, disease transmission modeling for malaria/TB), K-12 education tools in the US, India, and sub-Saharan Africa, and agricultural productivity for smallholder farmers. The partnership is led by Anthropic's Beneficial Deployments team and is central to the company's stated mission to extend AI's benefits beyond what markets alone will deliver.

- **[Claude Pushes Deeper Into Legal with 12 Practice-Area Plugins and 20+ MCP Connectors](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/bankruptcy-law/anthropic-pushes-deeper-into-legal-work-with-claude-updates)** - Anthropic unveiled 12 new AI plugins for specific legal practice areas — including corporate, regulatory, and employment law tools covering NDA triage, M&A governance, and hiring review — plus more than 20 MCP connectors linking to outside legal-tech software. Legal professionals have become Claude Cowork's most engaged knowledge-worker group, and analysts say this expansion is bigger than the earlier Cowork contract-redlining launch that sent legal tech stocks tumbling.

- **[Claude Code Ships Broad Update: Terminal Notifications, HTTPS Plugin Cloning, Improved Agent Controls](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude-code)** - Claude Code's latest release (first seen May 14) adds a terminalSequence field to hook JSON output enabling desktop notifications and window-title changes without a controlling terminal, CLAUDE_CODE_PLUGIN_PREFER_HTTPS for HTTPS-based plugin cloning in SSH-free environments, and ANTHROPIC_WORKSPACE_ID for workload identity federation scoping. The update also improves agent/session controls (claude agents --cwd), expands /feedback to include the last 24 hours or 7 days of sessions, and fixes a wide range of background, MCP, Windows, voice, and UI issues.

## OpenAI

- **[ChatGPT Gets Context-Aware "Safety Summaries" for Sensitive Conversations](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-recognize-context-in-sensitive-conversations/)** - OpenAI introduced "safety summaries" — short, time-limited factual notes generated by a dedicated safety-reasoning model — that let ChatGPT track risk as it evolves across a conversation, not just within a single message. In testing, the updates improved safe-response rates by 50% for self-harm scenarios and 52% for harm-to-others cases; they were developed with psychiatrists and psychologists from OpenAI's Global Physicians Network and may be extended to other high-risk domains like biology and cyber safety.

- **[OpenAI Engineering: Building a Safe Sandbox to Run Codex on Windows](https://openai.com/news/)** - OpenAI published an engineering post detailing the isolated execution environment they built to enable Codex to run code safely on Windows machines, covering sandboxing architecture and the security controls used for enterprise deployments. The post follows Codex's recent expansion to Windows and is part of OpenAI's broader effort to make its agentic coding tool trustworthy in regulated and enterprise environments.

## Google AI

- **[Google I/O 2026 (May 19–20): Gemini 4 / "Gemini Omni" Expected Imminently](https://io.google/2026/)** - Google I/O 2026 kicks off in four days (May 19 at 10 AM PT), with a new Gemini flagship model widely expected as the centerpiece announcement. A leaked UI string referencing "Omni" inside Gemini's video-generation tab suggests a unified text/image/video model is coming; the full event is also expected to cover Android 17, Android XR glasses, Aluminium OS (Google's new Android-based desktop OS), and deeper Gemini agentic capabilities.

- **[Gemini 3 Deep Think Gets Major Science-Focused Upgrade](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-deep-think/)** - Google released a significant upgrade to Gemini 3 Deep Think, its specialized scientific reasoning mode, achieving gold-medal-level performance on the written sections of the 2025 International Physics and Chemistry Olympiads and 50.5% on the CMT-Benchmark for advanced theoretical physics. The updated mode is now available in the Gemini app for Google AI Ultra subscribers, with a new early-access API program opening for researchers, engineers, and enterprises.

- **[Android Show Unveils Gemini Intelligence and Googlebooks Ahead of I/O](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/12/everything-google-announced-at-its-android-show-from-googlebooks-to-vibe-coded-widgets/)** - At the pre-I/O Android Show (May 12), Google detailed Gemini Intelligence — agentic AI for Android that can move across apps and complete multi-step tasks like booking reservations — alongside Googlebooks, a new laptop category from Acer, ASUS, Dell, HP, and Lenovo launching this fall with Google's "Magic Pointer" AI cursor and vibe-coded widget creation. Gemini in Chrome for Android also ships with auto-browse, form-filling via Personal Intelligence, and Rambler mode for cleaned-up voice dictation.

## Developer Tools & Vibe Coding

- **[Microsoft to Revoke Claude Code Licenses by June 30, Mandate GitHub Copilot CLI](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/microsoft-to-cut-claude-code-licenses-mandate-use-of-github-copilot-cli-by-june-30)** - Microsoft is phasing out most of its internal Claude Code licenses by June 30, requiring engineers across the Experiences + Devices team (Windows, Office, Teams, Surface) to transition to GitHub Copilot CLI, per an internal memo from EVP Rajesh Jha citing toolchain unification. The timing aligns with Microsoft's fiscal year-end cost reduction; Claude models will continue to power Copilot CLI and remain in Microsoft 365 via the existing Foundry agreement.

- **[Claude Code Weekly Limits Jump 50% Through July 13 in Competitive Push vs. Codex](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/2494/claude-code-weekly-limits-50-percent-anti-codex-anthropic-2026)** - Anthropic raised Claude Code weekly usage limits by 50% from May 13 through July 13, 2026, for all paid plans (Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise) in a direct competitive response to OpenAI's Codex on ChatGPT Plus. The boost narrows the weekly-cap gap between the two tools; analysts note Claude Code still consumes significantly more tokens per task but argue the quality and architectural-reasoning tradeoff remains meaningful for complex production codebases.

- **[Vibe-Coding Tool Market Splits Into Three Tiers as SpaceX/Cursor Deal Reshapes the Landscape](https://press.airstreet.com/p/state-of-ai-may-2026)** - The SpaceX $60B standing buyout option on Cursor (Anysphere) — deferred until after SpaceX's summer IPO — continues to reshape the vibe-coding market, with Cursor having crossed $2B in annualized revenue. The 2026 AI coding tool landscape has consolidated into three tiers: inline-suggestion tools (GitHub Copilot, ~42% market share), agentic CLI tools (Claude Code, OpenAI Codex), and AI-native IDEs (Cursor, Windsurf); packaging, context management, and execution loops are now more important differentiators than raw model quality.
